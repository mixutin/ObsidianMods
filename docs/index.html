MINECRAFT DUNGEONS II MODDING FAQ

Dungeons 2 mods, without the guesswork.

Where can I download Minecraft Dungeons II mods?

Use the Obsidian Mods Discover directory to browse verified Minecraft Dungeons II mod builds and install supported mods with one click.

Is there a Minecraft Dungeons II mod manager?

Yes. Obsidian Mods Manager supports Windows and Linux, including native Obsidian plugins, PAK/IoStore packages and UE4SS-compatible mods.

Does Obsidian Mods work on Linux?

Yes. The manager supports Linux builds and Minecraft Dungeons II running through Proton, alongside Windows.

How do I open the Obsidian Mods menu in-game?

Press F8. Obsidian Native Runtime renders the menu directly inside Minecraft Dungeons II and reports mod/runtime health.
